## Prompt

Studio product photograph of a single object floating against a seamless
backdrop. Nothing else is in the frame.

The object: one section of bamboo, reimagined as a precision manufactured luxury
product. It stands vertically, dead center of the frame, seen straight on, and
occupies about fifty five percent of the frame height. Its proportion is roughly
three to one, tall and slender. The body is a very dark green black satin
lacquer with a soft sheen, and the fine vertical fiber grain of real bamboo
still reads faintly through the finish as a subtle directional texture. Two
raised node rings divide the body into three even lengths. Each node ring is a
band of brushed champagne colored metal with a fine milled knurl around its edge
and a single hairline seam that suggests the ring could be opened. The top of
the culm is cut perfectly flat and is open, revealing a hollow interior with
clean concentric inner walls in a slightly lighter matte finish. The bottom is
cut perfectly flat.

The backdrop: a seamless infinite studio backdrop in light warm gray, with a
soft vertical gradient from near white at the top to light gray at the bottom
and a very subtle radial falloff toward the corners. There is no horizon line,
no visible floor edge, no backdrop seam, no wall, and no cast shadow on any
surface. The object floats. Only a very soft ambient occlusion sits directly
beneath it.

The lighting: a large soft key light from the upper left, a softer fill from the
right, and a thin rim light on both vertical edges of the object so that it
separates cleanly and crisply from the backdrop at every point along its
silhouette. A single controlled specular highlight runs vertically down the
lacquer body. The metal node rings catch a narrow horizontal specular band.

The composition: the object is centered and vertical. The entire left third and
the entire right third of the frame are flat, uninterrupted backdrop with no
detail, no texture, and no gradient banding. Generous empty backdrop above the
top of the object and below its base.

Style: high end product photography, sharp focus across the whole object, clean
edges, neutral color, restrained contrast, no post effects, no vignette, no
grain, no bloom.

Negative: no text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no logo, no watermark, no
signature, no captions, no labels, no user interface elements, no measurement
marks, no leaves, no foliage, no plants, no forest, no grove, no bamboo grove,
no green leaves, no environment, no landscape, no room, no wall, no table, no
desk, no floor, no ground, no horizon, no props, no second object, no group of
objects, no people, no hands, no faces, no animals, no panda, no rope, no
string, no ties, no water, no mist, no smoke, no fire, no reflections of a
scene, no mirror, no dramatic colored lighting, no neon, no cartoon, no
illustration, no 3d render look with plastic shading.
